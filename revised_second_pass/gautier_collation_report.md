## Current Confidence Distribution

- A: 68
- B: 19

`A` means the Greek witness is complete or nearly complete and the revised translation was checked closely against the Gautier packet. `B` means the reading is broadly strong but a localized textual, prosopographical, or apparatus issue remains. `C` means a Gautier packet exists but the letter still needs a full clause-by-clause audit. `D` means the surviving text is too mutilated or incomplete to justify a complete translation.

## Indexing And Recovery Decisions

- G001-G003 were recovered from Gautier I, not from the old local PG001-PG003 packets, which matched later old-edition material.
- G005/G006 were split correctly; old local G006 duplicated G047.
- G014 recipient was corrected to the bishop of Kitros, not Cyprus.
- G062 and G063 were separated into two distinct mutilated Gautier fragments and retained as D.
- G125 was recovered from Gautier II after the local PG084 packet failed.
- G134 was recovered from Gautier I as the genuine Demetrios/liturgy letter.
- The Christological Tivanios/Tigranes excerpt formerly filed as G134 was reassigned to G135.

## Substantive Correction Types

- Lost or inserted negation/agency: notably G069, G095, G114.
- False truncation or missing ending: notably G016, G051, G069, G093.
- Technical terminology: kanonikon, praktor, praktikon, zeugologion, episkepitites, chorion, aule, ospetion, harmostes, aer.
- Misread proper names or recipients: Kitros, Kritopouloi, Tivanios/Tigranes, Vardarion, Hagiosergitai.
- Classical/biblical allusions restored or tightened: Homeric horses, Sarambos, Actaeon, Antilochus, Titus 3:5, 1 Corinthians 11:30, the Dog-star/Sirius passage.
- Unsupported elegant paraphrase removed where the Greek did not justify it.
