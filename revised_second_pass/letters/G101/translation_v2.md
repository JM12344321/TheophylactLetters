## Revised English Translation

Crooked wood, my good John, never wishes to be straight. Nothing is better with me; it is worse. The enemy is the same and has not changed. Rather, like those beasts that grow more savage and almost mad because they have been driven into straits, he has used his rages without restraint, and the filthy creature plainly raves like a demon before all.

Only the power that drives out demons must therefore be invoked against one in such a state. When that power rebukes his legion, it will grant us travelers to walk the road of life without terrors. If it sends his burden upon the swine as well, and drives the Bulgarians who are fittingly ruled by him down the cliff that belongs to them, that is no matter, provided only that we ourselves walk our own road without hindrance and have our steps directed toward God. May you yourself be guided by him toward every good, leaping over the snares of the Evil One.
