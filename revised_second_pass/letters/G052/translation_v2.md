## Revised English Translation

For a long time we have both kept silence toward one another, most holy brother and master. You yourself will know the cause of your silence, and will tell it to us. As for me, my lips were closed first by the Frankish crossing, or incursion, or whatever I should call it. It dragged us wholly into anxiety and hauled us about so completely that we had no awareness even of ourselves. So deeply had we drunk from its bitter cup, and so strange was the trance into which we fell. Or rather, to say something in our own language, after drinking the dregs reserved for sinners we were troubled, we were shaken like a drunken man, and all our wisdom was swallowed up.

For this reason I was made dumb toward you; I was humbled, and kept silence from the good and pleasing conversations that I owed to your honor. This, then, became one fetter on my tongue. Another was that I did not find trustworthy letter-carriers. Most people are ready enough to receive letters, but numb and, as it were, handless when it comes to conveying them and delivering them to the person for whom they were written.

But since we have now grown accustomed to the Frankish outrages, and bear our hardships more easily than before - time is an obliging teacher of all things - and since we sometimes become ourselves again, and have found a letter-carrier who knows what he is doing, the devout monk under the most holy father lord Symeon, lord [...], behold, I will not let my lips be sealed. With mind, word, and letter I address and embrace the minister of the great Mind and Word, the one who guides from the letter toward the Spirit; and I ask how matters stand with your sacred and sanctified body.

If the tempter of the Lord is only gaping round about and does not touch the stone thrown at him, then may things be on the right-hand side. But if he troubles you also with things on the left, use even this, according to your Paul, as a weapon of righteousness. Through weakness you will show the power of God perfected in you, and you will make the life of Jesus manifest in a mortal and perishable body. The more the outer man is wasting away, the more the inner is being renewed and shows Jesus alive.

Shall I tell you my own case too? Everything bodily and everything spiritual stands for me on the left. One thing alone is on the right: my hope in your prayers. Grant them to me earnestly, and more warmly than usual, and I know that you will set my affairs in better balance.

Since, then, you are servant of the loving Master, and yourself a lover of brothers, show brotherly love upon brotherly love, so that he too may display you in heaven, in this and in all other matters, keeping for you the full reward, and even now setting you above every injury and every evil treatment.
