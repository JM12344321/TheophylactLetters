## Revised English Translation

You are not the cause of trouble for me. Certain people, perceiving that the man who is wholly noble and good in all respects belongs wholly to me, do not cease knocking at the door of my quiet, so that they may rouse it and have me bring them to you. I, being sluggish in other respects and at the same time clearly persuaded that you cannot do as much as the many suppose, hesitate at first to obey them. But when they apply force, I give way and promise them letters to your Splendor.

The man who now places before you this letter of my lowliness is one such person. He says that he is also a relative of the most philosophical and all-skilled Smyrnaios. If, then, you have room to help this man in some way, glory to the one who gave you the power to help. If not, glory to him even so; and you certainly remain without blame, since you wish all good things for everyone but do not have power running alongside your will.
