## Revised English Translation

A true friend is truly pained when he is deprived of the one he loves, especially when he is near the longed-for person and thinks he can almost grasp him, and his heart is scratched raw. Yet since "mind sees and mind hears," as the Pythagorean Epicharmos says, and since you yourself see us in this way, dearest and therefore most longed-for of men, I do not greatly blame the distance or the dismissal of what is longed for.

Love the greater thing when you have it, even if the lesser is not present. The greater thing, surely, is to see us by the mind, on which bodily sight also depends. "But the lesser should have been present too," you might say, "for in that way we would have possessed the things of friendship more richly." Yes, that is what I too would have wished, and it would have been much better. But God accomplishes many things contrary to expectation, and the things imagined were not fulfilled.

A Greek would say, "One must bear what is fated." Our Isaiah will pronounce more piously: "The holy God has confirmed it; who among human beings is able to scatter it?" As it seemed good to him, so it happened. By him may you be brought safely to your sweet homeland, and safely restored to those dearest to you as a treasure much longed for; and with them may you be preserved above every evil and injury.
