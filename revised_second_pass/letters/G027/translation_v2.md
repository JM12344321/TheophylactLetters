## Revised English Translation

Even if few men are remembered after they go down into Hades, I myself, at least, will lay up in those left behind by a dead friend the gratitude owed to him.

To the thrice-blessed hypertimos Psellos, whose tongue I rival only as a competitor admires excellence, I owe, as is fitting, thanks not easy to repay. I know that I have gained much from the man's Muse. Since I have no way to return favor to him in the whole matter that concerns him, I do what I can through your love for us.

The man who places this small letter of ours into your Splendor's hands is that man's grandson. Having had bitter experience of Fortune, he is in distress; therefore reconcile the tyrant to him somehow. If she merely nods to him, smiles on him, and embraces him - perhaps even drawing him into her lap - she will allow him to grasp good things.

Do not suppose that I write these things to you merely as a formality. May I have no benefit from words, nor from the will directed toward the salvation of my soul, if I do not write these things to you from the depth of my heart. I pity the young man, having once seen him among the prosperous; and I fear the soul of his grandfather, lest it reproach me for hardness and unyielding cruelty. I hesitate, for the moment, to say that it might charge me at judgment with the inhumanity shown to the goats on the left.

If he should appear to me in a dream and let loose that twelve-stringed tongue of his, do you think I could endure even the apparition of rebuke without trembling and disturbance? And if, besides all this, he should bring God in through David - who, though dead, defended Jerusalem, even if she was otherwise unworthy, and did so without bloodshed - what shall I suffer before those favors of his, pressed hard by the righteous man, by God, and by Psellos' tongue?

Rescue me from these necessities by placing this man sent to you in some service. Since many things are entrusted to you, it is certainly easy for you, if not to do more, at least to restore to us a man recommended by so many claims and judged worthy of help. The paths open to you are not narrow. May God keep you above every evil and every injury.
