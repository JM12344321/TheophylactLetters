## Revised English Translation

If God is love, as the disciple beloved in Christ divinely proclaimed, then I consider letters of love not a descent from God but an ascent toward God. They are that ladder seen by Jacob, with the words of union as its angels, and with the Lord standing upon it above all things, or before all things.

So do not accuse my lack of writing of being a wish to keep our union toward the better thing unbroken. Learn rather that, since love is woven more unbreakably for us through such conversation as is possible, we are bound more closely to God when I use letters. To say something from my old store, keep giving me your letters as a golden chain hanging down from heaven and lifting upward those who climb by it.

In such barbarous regions, where they wish to teach me nothing good, even a little outflow of wisdom descending to us seems to be the very fountain of nectar. See, then, that from now on you do not overlook us, dry as we are in a desert land, pathless and waterless. Such is the land to which we, envied and admired by many, have been condemned. I am thought blessed while I remain indoors; but when I open the doors, I am thrice-wretched.

These things I have never yet wept over without also gaining consolation. I think the true Comforter delays his descent toward us either to test longing or to show us unworthy of his grace. You know that upon Christ's genuine companions in substance he breathed even sooner than hope expected.

As for the matters about which you deemed me worthy, allow yourself to be admonished a little, you who legislate for many. For this too is wisdom: to know the measures of the order that perfects and is perfected. Do not wish to thrust yourself into divine matters, nor to be the tester of a human soul, a living thing by nature easy to change, to speak with your Plato. Be a friend up to that point; but what lies further inside, look upon as veiled in smoke and fire and guarded by the flaming sword.

Ah, your philosophical soul! How you received the admonition, how you blushed, how you reverenced divine things, how you shuddered. Remain so, then, and may the Lord keep you above every evil and injury.
