## Revised English Translation

From the first moment I tasted the honey of your goodness, my most magnificently distinguished lord, that same sweetness has lain on the mouth of my heart. Making my memories sweet, it never allows me not to remember the height of your Greatness. For this reason I always long to converse with you; and because in my unfortunate longing I have no way to cool the desire - distance wrongs me in the matter of cooling - I contrive it through letters. By this means I am with you and address you as is fitting.

Be healthy, then, in body and soul, and may the things around you prosper, so that through all things the stamp of blessedness may be manifest in you. May I hear about you things dear to God, appropriate to you, and drawn by us into prayer: honors for priests as for fathers; rest for the people; a return from alien territory as though a liberator had arrived; the subjection of barbarian lands, won more by goodness leading them on through goodness than by the spear. The spear incites flight, but goodness is a pledge held by the hand of love alone.

May rumor bring these things and more than these to us, and may we proclaim them with the loud-voiced mouth of speech, rejoicing in your successes and sharing joy with those who benefit. Let us at least have this good fortune: to look upon neighbors who are blessed and so extinguish the eyes of envy; for our own affairs are exceedingly bitter.

If, because of your goodness toward us, you wish to learn about them, the bearer of the letter will recount the circumstances of the letter. Since he is one of ours, he heard these things reported among us, suffered with us in soul, and held in memory what he heard. If ordered to speak, he will have a tongue that teaches the sufferings of his teacher.

As for us, being scourged by the Father, we await from him also our consolation, since he is called the Father of all consolation. As for you, if you make the Most High your refuge, you will not see evils coming near you, and no scourge will approach your dwelling, while you are kept above every corruption and injury.
