## Revised English Translation

You always kindle my longing, my most illustrious lord, since you are always reported to be greater than yourself in kindness. Setting ascents in your heart, you show us Jacob's ladder, making the present step a mounting-place for the future.

Therefore I pray that this ascent may be perfected for you, the ascent on which, as we believe, God is established. This is the end of every ascent: when it is reached, motion comes to perfect rest. For he says also to Moses, "You shall stand upon the rock."

Hasten, then, using your deliberate choice as a kind of impulse, and your steps will be directed by God. You will not strike your foot against a stone, whether we understand the stone as perceptible difficulty, hardness, and heaviness, or as the intelligible enemy, weighed down by pride and fallen from the order above.

For the good God, who is always believed to do good to the good, will not neglect your goodness. By him may you be kept above every evil and injury.
