## Revised English Translation

A man who loves lawsuits is a hard thing to get rid of. Such a man, my very great lord and protector, is Nicholas son of Boutos. Having attached himself to Michael Lampenos, son of Bessas, he formerly became the cause of countless damages to him, after finding the hands of Makrembolites very skillful at laying hold of other people's property.

This Nicholas, then, is so strange a man and so insensible to what is proper that, although he has been judged by the sebastos lord John Doukas, he does not remain within the things judged. Instead he still troubles the great just judgment of my most powerful lord and drags the said Michael, a man who lives by his hands, at the very season of agricultural work, into disputes and courts.

Let him be taught, then, through the uprightness of my lord, not to wrestle again in future with the judgments of sebastoi. The whole army, if questioned, will make the truth of the case plain. And the signed document itself issued by the sebastos speaks more clearly and more truly than any tongue.

Let informers and men worn out by litigation find black days in the times of my most powerful lord. Since Nicholas is ranked first in wickedness among them, let him also be first to enjoy the punishment for wickedness through your incomparable justice, under which all of us are guarded, we who have been given to it by God. May God guard you, and always, as I believe, continue to guard you as you do all his will.
