## Revised English Translation

My soul fails from longing for the dwellings of God. For this reason I would use wings, if it were possible, to come there and enjoy what I long for. But I seem to hear my sciatica crying out: "Stay, wretched man, in your bed."

Therefore you who are mine, announce to those who are mine and not mine, as though from my own mouth: "Bear with my weakness for a short time. It will soon set me, the burdensome man, far below; but it will unload you who are burdened. Do not use the power of wickedness to set the burden down. If you do, you will miss your intention - for the time is not favorable - and you will acquire the appearance of being evil, which follows from it. This is certainly a weight heavier than any other for those who are named from the good Christ, by whom they are thought both to have been formed and to be formed still."
