## Revised English Translation

I did wish, in writing also to your holiness, to find leisure for long words and to set before your sympathetic eyes a broader stage for my tragedies. But since we are poor even in this, learn from the short letter addressed to our common friend, the Grand Domestic, how great and what sort of things we are suffering.

If the instruction you receive from him is sufficient for your understanding, become a teacher of our affairs to others as well. Nor will you need a fuller account from me. When you have obtained my letter to the official in charge of petitions, which your disciple and my brother will certainly deliver to you, perhaps you will learn something more, and more nakedly.

However you come to know the circumstances in which I am placed, make every effort to transform them by your prayers. Since you preach long sermons in church, let it be a more merciful than just act to spend long words on behalf of a servant who has long been wearied. God will hear even your short words.
