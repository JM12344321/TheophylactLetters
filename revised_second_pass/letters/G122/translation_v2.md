## Revised English Translation

My most kind brother, most honored fellow bishop, has been taken up by the Lord and is truly blessed in that reception. We ourselves have been left behind in the place of grief, in the tents of darkness, in the valley of weeping, exposed to all the evil spirits of the place of grief. We lamented our brother as much as nature and the old Adam required; but we quickly looked away to the Word who renews nature, the second Adam and the first God, who also set laws for our mourning.

In him we trust: having taken up the brother who had become everything for us, he will not leave us henceforth without help, but will surely give strength to the weak in the ways he knows, just as he formerly strengthened us through our brother.

You too seek this very thing for us from him. Add also the request that you return more quickly to the place allotted to you, so that you may inspect the affairs of his flock which has been entrusted to you.
