## Revised English Translation

Having this alone as consolation in these evil days, all-holy brother and master - to address one another and to invoke one another's prayers as allies against those who grieve us - we greet your holiness through this little letter of ours. We ask you to send whatever help you possess to our weakness, as one would send a strong and tireless ally to a soldier who is already weak and worn out.

I mean your hands. If only they are lifted up, they will cast down Amalek, the one who stands in the way of God's people and blocks their straight passage. For he takes possession of every road and strives to humble the royal priesthood, the Lord's finest portion. You are not ignorant who this is: the apostate from the beginning, the arrogant one, the tyrant of the world.

Against him we call you, man of God. May it come to pass that through you both we and all who are tyrannized by him find relief from our griefs. Perhaps you yourself too will gain something great from your prayers, not only because, now suffering with our troubles, you will rejoice with us when they are again well, but also because, having yourself privately become caught in the many thorns of afflictions, you too need their removal; and when that happens, you will carry off no small benefit.
