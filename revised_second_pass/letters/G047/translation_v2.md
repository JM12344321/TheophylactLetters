## Revised English Translation

"No word about me?" You know the comic fragment well. But this rule of yours, which has received the lot of doing good to many, forgets its own purpose when it comes to me and deprives me alone of your good things.

For to everyone else it pours nectar through your tongue like a fountain; but noble as it is, it leaves me dry and, as the poets say, without a libation. What advocate will it put forward for itself? How will it not be convicted on every charge when I summon it for doing me wrong? Does it rely confidently on my rusticization among barbarians?

But, wonderful judge, since I am arranged under your laws, I trust not in words but in facts. These facts, surely, have not been made rustic. Steal back my tongue a little, then, and let some nectar fall drop by drop; or sharpen that tongue even more on your own behalf. As for me, I will not endure failing to accuse negligence toward friendship.

I am formed for the case by a sharp advocate: the facts themselves. Even if my servant is weak and my horses slow, Diomedes knows how to save Nestor.
