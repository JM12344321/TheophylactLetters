## Revised English Translation

My very great lord and protector: you have done many good things in the midst of our land, refreshing both Prespa and Diabolis with the spirit of uprightness and just judgment which you displayed against those who wronged them. Yet your great hand will accomplish nothing less, indeed perhaps something greater, if you take pity on the bishopric of Diabolis. It is in great danger, but through a small act of guidance it can escape that danger.

What is the danger? It has nearly become without psalmody and without light, because it has become without episcopal oversight. It lies open like that vine in David, once rich in branches and now plundered by all who pass by. It does not have its bishop remaining there; for he cannot endure giving his back to scourges and his cheeks to blows. And when the bishop flees because of the terrible oppressors, it is not reasonable to expect anyone else to remain with the Church. The rest of the body stands together with the head.

Indeed, those who were given to him by the sigillion of our mighty and holy lord and emperor, and those who once sat around the Church, left their ancestral holdings for this same reason, and after thickening themselves with the density of the woods they are hiding there. Because of this, neither deacon nor priest - alas for our evils - has remained in the most beautiful of the Bulgarian churches.

I went out to inspect it. Seeing it, I mourned; having mourned, I asked the cause; having asked, I learned; having learned, I teach, reporting it by this humble letter to my most powerful lord.

The danger, then, is this. What is the guidance needed? Light and weightless: precisely the stretching out of a single hand. If you are pleased, our great lord and protector, to add your confirming sigillion to those who were given to the bishop by our mighty and holy lord and emperor, and who had once sat close to the Church but then fled because of molestations, they will trust in it and come out of the thicket. The Church will sprout again; it will be lit; it will be filled with psalmody.

Do you see that the danger is very great? Do you see that the help is easy, accomplished by only three fingers? I beg, then, and through this present letter as through a body I fall before you: have mercy on what was once the most blessed of churches, the church which that most Christian Boris, king of Bulgaria, built as one of the seven catholic churches. He built it; you renew it. Then may an upright spirit be renewed in your inward parts by God, who raised up and renewed our fallen tent and nature.

May that God also grant effective power to your right hand in every work, military and civil, making you a most exact imitator of our mighty, holy, and Christ-loving father and lord.
