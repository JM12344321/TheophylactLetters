## Revised English Translation

Who would give me leisure for words, my most venerable protector, as much leisure as I desire, so that I might fit my tongue to the glory of your achievement? Who would assemble for me a chorus of rhetoricians, so that, becoming their leader as once I was, I might give them the signal and together with them complete the dance of speech?

For you have shown yourself a helper in the afflictions that have found us so severely, sent as a defender by the One who sends help from his holy place. You have crushed the heads of the dragons upon the waters; you have shattered the head of the dragon. Even if it now writhes with its tail and seems alive, in the judgment of those who judge rightly it has been broken. As for us, through you, who received power to tread upon all the power of the enemy, we have taken back life, and we laugh at the one who boasted beyond measure and said: "With my hand I shall seize the inhabited world; I shall wipe away the sea; and I shall carry off all people as though they were abandoned eggs."

But you, Lord, have not abandoned those who seek you. You looked down from heaven and saw the sons of those put to death; you had pity on us. Upon the son of man whom you strengthened for yourself, upon the man of your right hand, you sent forth your light and your truth; you increased your majesty upon the sebastos; you taught his hands for battle and his fingers for war; you shattered the arms of the sinner and broke iron bars. You brought out those who were bound and said to the lawless, "Do not act lawlessly," and to those who provoke, "Do not lift up the horn."

You see, most venerable lord, how I have hymned the gifts of God concerning you as far as I could in the words of God. For it is not fitting to diminish things beyond human measure by mere human language. Therefore preserve in goodness your imitation of God. Never wish evil to anyone. Show goodness toward all, and especially toward those who were first shown mercy by you, such as we are. You would grant us the greatest favor if you would deem our kinsmen in Euripos worthy of mercy and show those in Hellas that, in the eyes of you, the most venerable protector of all, the archbishop is worthy of some considerable regard.

Fulfill my joy, then, by taking up all the matters concerning my people. And may God exalt you still more, glorifying you because through your goodness you glorify him each day; and may your enemies lick the dust.
