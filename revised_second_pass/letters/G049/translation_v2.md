## Revised English Translation

You, good man, are so anxious on behalf of my words, lest they be caught in falsehood, that you have hastened to confirm them by the deeds themselves. For I was always accusing you as negligent, and you were always rejecting the name, as Autolykos would not have rejected Odysseus.

But, blessed man, do not honor friendship in this way. What you would wish for yourself, wish also for the one who is thought to be loved. If the things you undertook have been completed, good. If not, at least this will lay up a very great favor with us: that you not also lose the dikaiomata granted to you.

Worthless as I am, I was asking another thing of you as well: when the decree concerning Vodena is made, show it to lord Gregory Taronites and ask from him a note to the man acting in Beroia, indicating what he must do. If the decree has been made, the request concerning this still stands. If not, see that, being so swift, you do not also run the risk of stumbling. We have learned from the proverb that speed is unsafe.

May the Lord keep you from every snare of the devil and from negligence toward good things.
