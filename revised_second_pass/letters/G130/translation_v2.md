## Revised English Translation

I rejoice at not receiving letters from you, for I infer that you have affairs in hand beneath which there is some profit. If I hit the mark and do not shoot aside, then in my own sorrows I have this much consolation from your affairs. But if I am unlucky in your affairs too, then the Evil One comes upon us with a double spear. God alone, the good and mighty one, will stand against him. May you too be guarded by him above that one's evil contrivance.
