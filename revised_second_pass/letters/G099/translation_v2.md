## Revised English Translation

Great thanks to logos, because he has produced for us such sons, preserving his own marks exactly. Such a son you too have proved to be for me, dearest one, and this in days so evil and so fertile in monsters, like those ox-born creatures with human fronts in Empedocles. You fight for us with all your spirit and repay splendidly the returns owed for midwifery and nurture in logos, even if you are not wholly sufficient to drive out from here the death-bearing dogs who devoured me and divided me no less than Actaeon's own hounds, which he had raised, divided him.

What we improvised against their barking so that it might be heard by the emperor, you will certainly read. May it belong to God, the Father of truth, to illuminate the imperial mind in these matters too, to drive away the darkness of falsehood, and to set the facts before him. In this way the arm of sinners will be broken and the righteous man directed aright. As things are now, while their hand is doing whatever it wishes, there is danger that even the righteous man may be twisted and learn wickedness, if the rod of sinners falls more violently upon his allotted portion.

These things must be entrusted to him in whose justice and love for mankind we have trusted: to his justice, because we are conscious of none of the things for which we are maligned; to his love for mankind, because we know that he follows his own laws and overlooks the rest of our lawlessness. Use, however, the arrows of your tongue, which you have received from logos to discharge at the right moment. Strike the Ethiopians, my Antilochus, standing in front of your Nestor.
