## Revised English Translation

The gifts of divine men, my most venerable lord and protector, ought to be without regret, just as God's gifts are. Since, then, your eminence first granted to us, from the God-crowned and mighty emperor, and bestowed upon the Church a kind of divinity of authority, if I may speak so - I mean the complete authority over the priests of the neighboring towns, burdened only by the tax of the zeugnelion and set above all other acts of molestation - why should you now change so great a grace, my holy lord and protector?

Even if we had moved in some way against your glory and had stained the good already done through ingratitude, even then one ought not overturn a favor done to the house of God; for the Church is both called and believed to be God's house. But if we inscribe you as lord and benefactor, for we have not become so mad as to prove ungrateful and respond perversely to your kindness, you see how easily the sigillion of the most venerable Komnenos has been overturned by Komnenos himself. That touches, in some small way, both reproach and boldness.

Yet it is not impossible to place a salve upon the wound. Indeed the salve is easier than the blow: a heavenly salve, signed beneath and defined by your venerable hand for the representative official, that the priests should be released from every molestation and servitude belonging to the praetorium in regard to lay exactions, and that whatever has been taken from priests, clerics, or any others should be returned.

I must experience a good reversal, or rather one ordered in person through the great protection of my lord: if any of the men said to be intermediaries have priests and misuse them for their private services, they should no longer possess this tyranny over the archbishops subject to them. For these men have also stirred up the common people to accuse me before my lord because of the priests, wishing to harvest the priests for themselves and breaking the privilege of the Church for their own gains.

May Jesus Christ, our first high priest, keep my most venerable lord in every sound condition of body and spirit. And do not disdain to receive a small blessing, itself complete in number: one hundred salted fish, [a final phrase in the PG/OCR is damaged].
