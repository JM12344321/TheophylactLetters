## Revised English Translation

You have put the capstone on the afflictions surrounding me, my surpassingly brilliant son in the Lord, by saying that you too are hemmed in by afflictions. The medicine that I compounded for myself through the herbs of discourse, knowing it useful for you also, I shall send when I can seize the right moment. For now, composing this little letter for you in daylight, I had no way to take the medicine from the box. It is not simply open, nor exposed to everyone, but needs a more painstaking hand to be opened.

For the present I can accomplish only this much for you: after winter there is certainly spring, and all that belongs to spring. The phoenix, child of the Sun, rises even after being reduced to ashes - to say nothing more divine and more our own.
