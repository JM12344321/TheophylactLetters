## Revised English Translation

When I am thirsty to learn your affairs more clearly, my surpassingly brilliant son in the Lord, you send me murky drops, from which I do not heal my thirst. Write to me, then, more plainly: what condition you are in, and whether it is worthy of the hopes on which we were nourished.
