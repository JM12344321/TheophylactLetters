## Revised English Translation

But no word about me? You know well the little comic scrap.

Yet this office of yours, which has been allotted the task of doing good to many, forgets its own purpose in my case and deprives me alone of your benefits. For through your tongue it pours nectar in streams for everyone; but noble as it is, it leaves me dry, and, as the poets say, unwatered.

What advocate, then, will it appoint for itself? How will it not be convicted on every count when I summon it for wronging me? Does it take courage from my being made rustic among the barbarians? But, admirable mistress of the courts, since we are marshaled under your laws, we put our trust not in words but in facts; and the facts, surely, have not become rustic.

So steal a little of your tongue for me as well, and drip some of the nectar; or else sharpen that tongue still further in your own defense. For I, at least, will not endure failing to bring a charge against negligence in friendship. I am furnished with a sharp advocate: the facts. Even if my servant is weak and my horses are slow, Diomedes knows how to save Nestor.
