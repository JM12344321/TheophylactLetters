## Revised English Translation

I greet you, the head dear to me, most honored brother. Not only this: I also ask you to pray on behalf of our humility, that, once delivered from the evil of the present age, we may one day breathe again with a refreshment more divine and more fitting for Christians. For now, buried partly under our own private troubles and partly under common ones, we have no way to secure even the slightest chance to lift our heads again.

So completely has Satan once and for all mastered, simply speaking, all who conduct public affairs. Now it is time for us to say the Apostle's words, not as they were spoken, but as our circumstances dictate them: each of these men does not bear the sword in vain, but in order to slaughter, despise, and make childless. Nor are they slow about it, nor do they grow slack in the work. As though each expects to win favor the more he harms Christians, each tries to get ahead of the other in doing evil and to outdo him.

For these things, and against these things, we call on your prayers: may they make their sword duller. Or rather, since a duller sword, by prolonging the cutting, does more harm, may your prayers break it completely. Pray, then, with both hands, as the saying goes, and God will grant relief to his clergy.
