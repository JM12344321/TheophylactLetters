## Revised English Translation

I am calling a horse into a plain when I urge you to help the just. For that reason my letter to you on this matter is brief. Show me, then, by helping the present bearer, that you are the horse: Balios, if you like, or Xanthos, or else the horse of Hieron.

## Consequential Changes From First Pass

- Restored the horse-proverb opening and the classical references to Achilles' horses Balios and Xanthos and to Hieron's Olympic horse.

## Source And Revision Notes

- Gautier notes that the text is transmitted only by Chisianus R.IV.12 and is anepigraphic.
- The opening proverb is used for urging someone to do what he would naturally do anyway.
