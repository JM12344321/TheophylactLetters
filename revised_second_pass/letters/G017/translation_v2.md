## Revised English Translation

Now I know what sort of sun we had, and were filled with, and now the graces of spring are sweet to us. For you turned your face away and we were all troubled. We long for your gentleness; we seek your moderation of mind, your sociability with all, your graciousness. Ah, how much we lament! Know that small and great alike proclaim your beneficent and large-giving hand, your honoring and humane disposition, and your reverence and deference toward those dedicated to God.

You were manifestly given to us by God because of his virtues, then sent away because of our sins; but for others God preserves you as a savior, a supplier of every kindness. God, like a master builder taking a naturally gifted pupil into great works, gives these matters to you as material, so that in them you may display your skill. I trust in the virtues of your family, virtues without rival, that in everything you will be proclaimed more skillful and more proven than all.

May you again do good to the affairs of Bulgaria by divine economies through which your own matters are conducted. Even now you have not altogether abandoned us. For even if the sun moves from the northern parts farther south, it still warms and gives life according to measure to all things that are not near it. In this way we too, in the region of Pelagonia, enjoy your rays.

We would enjoy them more if the little place called Mogila, where our ancient court also stands, were given to your guardian, the Theotokos, by your venerable and worshipful sigillion, and if Romanos Ostrakoromos there, who has fattened himself from the Church and now rages against her, were restrained by the threats of my lord. Let instructions concerning him be given both to the military officer and to the other men, so that no one may be given room against us, who pray always for your good repute, who always proclaim your deeds, and who possess a tongue for thanksgiving inferior to no one's, not even, dare I say, Romanos' or many others'.

But you who have gathered into yourself the good things of the blessed Doukas family, remain for me in that goodness, or rather set ascents and advances in it, if there is any measure beyond what you have already achieved. Do not depart from the place given by God, I mean the beneficent and good-doing disposition, and the moderation of mind in all things, which has exalted you and will exalt you further, while we lowly people ask [the ending is damaged].
