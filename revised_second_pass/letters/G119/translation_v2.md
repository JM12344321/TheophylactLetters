## Revised English Translation

Although we make a small gift of fish, not worthy of your Greatness, my all-venerable lord and greatest helper, we do so knowing that you are discerning and exceedingly reverent toward the things of God, since in both respects you take after your father. From your discernment you will reckon that we possess no authority at all over the lake under us. From your reverence you will receive these small things as a blessing of our Lady, the Mother of God and Theotokos. Any blessing whatever, however great or small it may be, is to be so regarded.

For this reason we have made the gift with confidence. If the archbishop knows your character correctly, you will show it by receiving these things gladly. If not, I shall count even this among my sins. Nevertheless I trust that the first will happen, and that you will admit these things as the blessing of the unwedded bride of God. May she be your guardian through all life, keeping you above every corruption and injury.
