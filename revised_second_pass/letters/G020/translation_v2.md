## Revised English Translation

Who is more blessed than we, my most illustrious lord, if we should benefit so great a soul? If, when we do something even for the least, we believe that we do it for God, as our great God and Savior taught, then when we lay down a treasure of benefit for a very great soul, certainly such as yours is, how much should we rejoice and put on the garment of spiritual gladness! It is almost as though we already held in our hands heavy and abundant rewards for this from the One who gives a word to those who proclaim good news, repays each person the wage according to his own labor, and sets over so many cities those who multiply their minas.

You surely know the Gospel parable. It shows the grateful servants blessed and enriched, and the servant who truly belonged to ingratitude reproached and cut off because he wrapped the mina in the cloth of deadness and idleness and did not deposit it with the bankers.

Every human being is a banker once he has received the gift of reason, the stamp of the royal image. Therefore we especially, who have been set in the ministry of the word, must deposit with every person honored with reason the Master's coin, the silver tested by fire, the pure oracles of God, so that at least our part may be free from blame. From then on, the whole weight will rest upon the bankers' trade.

I, then, deposit with you a very small coin. Multiply it for me, and you will have treasure in heaven from the One who promises to give hidden and invisible treasures, to speak according to holy Isaiah.

And what is my small coin? "Do not be jealous of evildoers." Do this and you will live; and the grace of the Lord Jesus be with your spirit.
