## Revised English Translation

I, who was invoking your most venerable eminence as mediator even for others, was feeding on empty hopes, and necessity laughed at me as I was being led away from the pasture. For I myself was soon about to be deprived of what was my own, and so hotly that the evil would be stronger than every help.

My lord, I found that the village which the Church had possessed from ancient times, subject to no practical exaction, had been taken away by the emperor and torn from the Church, just like the properties of all the archons. Then, finding it under a small servitude by the collector of the day, I held it, like many others, under that slight consolation. But if this is being requested from our lord for him to grant to whomever he wishes, I do not resist, though it would have been just that no one else be preferred to the Church by the pious family of the Doukai, especially when the Church had possessed the village from the beginning.

Concerning the village I say nothing more; perhaps it will be taken away from all the others too. But concerning the Church's lodging-house and courtyard, I do not believe that any such command was issued by our lord. Nevertheless, behold, this man of ours has been sent. If it seems good to your nobility, let this petition be reread: on what grounds are we, the servants of the emperor, driven from the land which until now we cultivated believing it to be ours? If it now seems not to be ours, we shall certainly pay the zeugologion, with your eminence knowing that this will also run to everyone else.

When our affairs are scrutinized, when the splinter is seen and the gnat is strained out, the beam must not remain unseen, nor the camel be swallowed. You have authority. Do what you command concerning the courtyard. Let it be set on fire, let it be burned down, let a great torch be lifted up, so that by its light these unseen matters may become visible.

May the Theotokos, whose are both the land and the courtyard now being molested, keep you above every molestation. This, as you know, is our prayer.
