## Evidence Notes

- Gautier's title identifies the recipient as Theophylact's brother Demetrios.
- The letter answers two liturgical questions: the Lenten shoulder-kiss of the priest and the elevation of the presanctified bread while covered by the aer.
- Major allusions include Deuteronomy 25:4, Luke 11:10, Philippians 2:8, Matthew 17:2, Galatians 5:24, John 12:32, Colossians 2:15, John 19:34, Matthew 28:18, John 6:63, John 4:24, 1 Corinthians 13:12, Hebrews 10:1, Colossians 3:1-2, Philippians 3:20, Ephesians 2:19, Matthew 6:2/6:16, Genesis 18:27, and Daniel/Susanna 42.
