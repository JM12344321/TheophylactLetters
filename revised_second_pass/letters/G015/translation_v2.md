## Revised English Translation

Excellent news, that an addition has been made to the flock of Christ. Excellent news, that the divine boundary-lines have been widened for you, and that a people being created for you praises the Lord in no small way. My spirit rejoiced at the conversion of the Armenians.

Their churches should not be closed. Rather, after you have performed the service which we perform at the dedication of a church, and have anointed the walls crosswise with chrism, hand them over to Orthodox priests; and in this way make everything useful for those who have converted from the Armenians.

If some of their priests too have come over to the Orthodox faith, it is better that they be appointed among the laity. If, however, they cling to the priesthood and are otherwise blameless, ordain them yourself. Only their liturgy must be examined exactly. If it is unchanged and no different from that of Chrysostom, it should be served. If it has been corrupted, it must be changed. The rest of the psalmody should also be examined.

I know that for these matters you will call for my presence. Yet I suspect that after Pascha our lord the sebastos will come either to the places near us or to us ourselves. Since this is the way we are accustomed to proceed, there is no need for my presence there. For now, then, write to us about this matter. We shall receive your report with pleasure and proclaim it to all the more gladly, because you were taken captive by those who were supposed to be friends.

And yet, though you had only one horse left to you, you sent it to the Church for its use. Even if it is not entirely suitable for the purpose for which we need it, your gratitude toward the Church is great, and I pray earnestly for you because you know who gave you what you possess. Most people do her injury even when they add something.

Tell me whether you have the full rite which we perform over those who convert from the Armenians. If you do not have it, we shall copy it out, and either bring it or send it. Do not cease, brother, to take thought for the psalmody in the Church, and in general for the good order of the house of God. May God shelter you from every enemy.
