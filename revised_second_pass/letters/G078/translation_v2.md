## Revised English Translation

Nothing is now troublesome to me, because I have received the address of the sweetest mind and tongue of all. It is not temporary, like a lofty blow of air that, at the same moment as its formation, also receives its dissolution, but solid and lasting. For carried on writing from your Magnificence to my lowliness, it remains with me, so that I may be addressed by you whenever I wish, simply by unrolling the scroll. That is a ready way for me to shake off the burdens that lie upon me.

When in reading I go through your trophies as well, I drink nothing other than that drug which makes grief forgotten - or, to speak more fittingly, since the land of the Colchians now enjoys you, by the art of Medea of Colchis my old age is scraped away and my youth is renewed. You see in how many common misfortunes I myself prosper through you and through your successes, which surpass even those of the ancients that are sung in hymns.

I also took the timing of the reception of your greeting as a favorable omen. For as I was going to great Achilleios, so that I might celebrate with the festival, your letter was handed to me on the road. There too you embitter those who afflict us: the servants of greed, the enemies of the Gospel's promise, the polluted ones of the Christian order. Send against those accursed men this one missile that you possess, against men who prove the belly of all-receiving Hades too narrow and boast in their excess.

May Jesus, who subdued even that great sea-monster, bring them beneath his own hand. May he touch their belly and contract its great mass, dispersing the swelling, whether as he administered the belly of Zacchaeus or as he dealt otherwise with the incurable rulers among the Jews, against whom he sent the stern physicians, the Romans, cutting and burning.

For you, may Jesus set your road blameless, always before your eyes at your right hand so that you may not be shaken. As you receive him genuinely and richly in the poor and in those otherwise dedicated to him - or rather in those who image him - and as you show love by works, may he make your arms a bow of bronze. Girding you with strength, may he scatter through you the nations that love wars, breaking the bow, shattering the weapon, and burning shields with the fire that boils from the activity of your zeal. When those who do evil draw near against you and the camp of the Lord's enemies arrays itself, may they themselves grow weak and fall out of their plots. May you pursue them and overtake them; may you crush them and tread like mud in the streets those who have chosen the broad way of godless Muhammad.

I do not merely pray these things for you; I am persuaded that they will happen. For God is not unjust, so as not to give the crown of victory to one summoned from abundance by purity of soul and nobility of hands. Already I shall strike up your victory-song: David has struck in his tens of thousands - the gentlest of men today and the fiercest, the one who has struck down the bear of desire and the lion of blameworthy anger. May the Lord, who loves righteousness and beholds uprightness, not scatter his mercy from you, but always keep you above every corruption and every injury.
