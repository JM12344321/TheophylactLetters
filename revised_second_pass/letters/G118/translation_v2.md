## Revised English Translation

If bodily strength and the mildness of the season allowed my lowliness to travel, my all-venerable lord and greatest helper, I would quickly run up to your Eminence and render you the prostration owed. But now - since the things I have mentioned do not allow me even to step over the threshold of my room - I use the present letter as mediator of my reverence.

Surely you will receive the reverence offered through a letter just as you would receive that offered through the body, since I am one and the same man who would bring the one and now brings the other to you. I am clearly persuaded that you will receive it - rather, you have already received it. You look upon me most graciously, as you are accustomed to do, and ask what I need.

Shall I tell you my need, then? Or should I be ashamed, when I have you as harmostes over the affairs of Vardarion and possess an estate in Vardarion, to rouse you more humanely to take up its cause? You are always a mediator for my weakness before others who can help me. This is what likelihood requires. Nevertheless, since we are accustomed to give wings with encouraging acclamations even to those who run well, I too bring before you, as a kind of winging for this very purpose, my request that you take up those of ours there as your own servants.

Show everyone that you have as a paternal inheritance a kind disposition toward the archbishop, and that you yourself have multiplied it many times over, so that God, to whom you grant favor in whatever you grant to me, may award you many times your father's glory and show you more blessed in all good things, keeping you above every injury and corruption.
