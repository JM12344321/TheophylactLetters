## Revised English Translation

Am I perhaps bold in your eyes, most revered sir, and excessive in my frankness, because before I have either seen you or been seen by you, I at once set myself up as your adviser? Or am I admirable in my simplicity, because I share my wealth even with acquaintances and give you a part of what is in me? I myself think the second. If anyone supposes the first, he is truly unadmonished and has never received any good from reason.

I say, then, that since you are young and have received supervision over human beings, and since your heart is easy to impress, you must be fortified on every side and look around at everything with more eyes than all-seeing Argus watching that entrusted cow - whatever the myth means by these things - so that vice may not escape notice and imprint its marks upon your soul. Youth by itself is a daring and active thing; and when the power of office is present too, it "speaks with a sword," as they say. But if you have with you the great and formative Word, these things will become material for greater reputation.

Then the heat-producing force of youth, fanned by the breath of authority, will run through affairs carrying a saving torch for those baptized in poverty. I count you blessed, because almost before you have entered the theater of life you have found an easy occasion for frankness toward the great emperor and ruler: kindness toward those you govern.

Let the greatness of your nobility know well that nothing so blends a human being with God as good character and love for mankind. The wonder is that we are not asked for anything great or hard, but only to recognize nature and to answer the sympathy required by nature. May you become and be proclaimed such a man. Treasure up this wealth rather than the countless talents that others gather out of tears and mourning, riches that already here show the dissolution into which they will be assigned in the future.

Present yourself as a god, in imitation, to all who need humane care, and especially to those who have been privately dedicated to God. In this way most of all you will show that you have been eager to serve him. For someone who cares for your dogs also serves you; but if he grants something more to those who serve you privately and more intimately, he shows more clearly his fellow-feeling toward you.

Hear the whole matter as the conclusion of my speech: gouge out the eyes of envy. You will not turn away the tongue of blame if you appear disobedient to the law of the present days. May the God of goodness and justice keep you above every corruption and every harm.
