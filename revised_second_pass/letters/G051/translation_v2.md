## Revised English Translation

Even if the enemy works wickedness in God's holy place, and shows himself again and again more active than himself, most honored lord and our father, always setting ascents of wickedness in place, still the Lord of powers is with us, pulling down that enemy's structures and showing his evil devices to be vain.

And why should you not be with us too, having been made to dwell among us, unworthy as we are, by your holy words and prayers through your own kindness? Do not cease preparing him to remain in the places where you have already made him dwell, so that, being with us all the days we shall have - that is, as we are illumined by the light of your words and prayers until the present evil age is completed in us - he may keep us free from the night of evil deeds.
