## Revised English Translation

Pour active antidotes for me against the illnesses that attack; do not spare the cups when you pour in your God-given panacea - especially the chief one, the medicine made through viper flesh. In Thessalonica, fullness is expected for me; at the Churches, a chorion of the archbishopric, emptying. From both comes slackening and paralysis. Surely I do not invoke my Asclepius in vain against so great a disturbance.

That much is not in vain. But see whether someone might say that I am rhapsodizing in vain, weaving riddles when I should state the need more plainly. I will do just that. Learn the matter. We have a little house in which we lodge and deposit the things we need. Some of those under our all-venerable lord are trying to fill it. Head off this filling for me. It would be dreadful if others found rest in what is ours while we ourselves wandered around other people's doors.

The estate of the churches, unless it remains unharmed, will be emptied - know this well. Stop this emptying for me too. The manner of stopping it is this: for the moment say nothing at all about the estate, neither more nor less. But when our people come to you in the estate, either because they are near to being injured or because they are already being injured, then spare neither word nor deed on our behalf.

I have told you my affairs. Give me reason to speak to others of hopes worthy of you, hopes which the Word who orders you plants firmly in me. My brother greets you, but from Taenarus, by which he is being led down to Hades, guided by wasting disease. Against that guidance I ask from God an obstacle.
