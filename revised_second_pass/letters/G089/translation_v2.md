## Revised English Translation

My holy lord, in the letter recently sent to your Greatness I lamented in tragic tones the dark gathering of clouds around me; and, being hard pressed by it, I all but wrote the letter in tears. Now I have seen a downfall of hail too, pitifully lamenting our affairs. I do not know how to mourn the calamity worthily. The very strength of my tongue has been broken, since, being stripped bare, it lay exposed like a target for missiles.

Nevertheless I am able to say this one thing: truth has altogether been outstripped by falsehood, and slander, crowned, marches in splendid triumph against justice upon a purple chariot. For this reason many among us are weak and sick, and a fair number sleep. Me - O earth and sun! - me the stage of the present life displays as being mocked by two Bulgarians. Where is Oedipus, then, and his famous riddles?

But you, as disciple, friend, and ally of truth, help her in her danger as far as you can. This will happen if you are pleased to speak on her behalf into the ears of the emperor and your brother. It is dreadful, dreadful indeed, for dead dogs to rise against a living lion when he has been bound by divine and imperial bonds and had his terrible roars cut off - the very roars by which alone he could frighten them. It is dreadful for Samson to be shaved by the razor of slander and then handed over to foreigners, so that even the eyes with which he saw heavenly things are gouged out, and he grinds the grain that feeds those godless men, always turning around the same things and cut off from progress.

I no longer have strength to say more; I am so confounded even by the bare memory of what is happening. But if the emperor's justice, through your most powerful conversation on our behalf, commands restraint upon the outrages now dared against us, perhaps those of us who have been shaved will grow our hair again. Perhaps, pulling down the house of outrage upon the foreigners who revel over us, we may bring the long labor to an end, and ourselves escape not only shame but death as well. In return for this, may you be kept above every corruption and injury by the just God who is indignant at wickedness.
