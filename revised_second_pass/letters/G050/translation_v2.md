## Revised English Translation

The Achridians hear my song as donkeys hear a lyre. Thus I am useless and talkative beyond what is fitting. If they knew who Hippocrates was, they would even summon him against me, so that, mixing drugs, he might free me from madness. As it is, I have at least enjoyed this much from the ignorance of my Leibethrians: they do not cause me the trouble that comes from drinking medicine.

May you love wisdom for me, and be loved by wisdom; and by it may you be kept above every evil and injury. Those whom you greeted greet you in return, almost each one dripping his soul into the greeting.
