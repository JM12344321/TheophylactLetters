## Revised English Translation

Good John has deafened my ears, most radiant son in the Lord, narrating your qualities with applause. What good thing you showed him I do not know very clearly; but his words make you out to be that cloud which once rained gold for the Rhodians. If I did not know him to be very sensible and therefore not a liar, I would not have yielded to the novelty of his praises. They surpass the goodness of the present race - whether I should call it an iron race or a clay one, I do not know.

Whence, then, have you appeared to us, such a bright star in the present night of wickedness, as Pindar says? Whence, blessed man? Loosen the bonds of my perplexity.

And yet the solution is near. Philosophy, benefactress of human beings, humanizes even the bestial and the brutish no less than Circe restored Odysseus' companions from swine to human form by applying another drug to each. This humane philosophy fashioned such a habit in you and planted a heavenly plant in alien earth, to speak with her and your Plato.

Hold fast, then, to goodness, through which, although you walk upon the earth, you draw near to God and set your head against heaven. This is the truly philosophical person. Let no one merely chatter to me about celestial matters; let no one explain condensation, roasting, and flying; let no one boast of the greater Alpha and then, when something must be done and he must come down, surpass Sarabos, the Athenian shopkeeper of old. Such a man neither knows beings as beings - otherwise he would have attached himself to what truly is - nor practices death.

Do not be such a one. Then may God the Word, the true Wisdom of God, illuminate for you still more and more the light of knowledge and keep you above every evil and injury.
