## Revised English Translation

The priests in Polog have obtained no dispensation or relief at all, even though you, my lord, wrote to us by your revered note to the man who was acting as representative and granted them relief. They are still dragged into local guard services and food-supply obligations, even though the chrysobull commanded that they be exempt from both these sordid public services and the food-supply obligations.

For my part, what just claim I had, I have laid before my lord. If some people are obstructing your goodness, know that they are enemies of the good, preventing your pure soul from rendering to God the things of God. Since you have him as ally in everything, do not allow those consecrated to him to be counted together with the common people. And may he himself guard you in times of war and peace, as you are at peace with him.

## Consequential Changes From First Pass
