## Revised English Translation

You know Antilochus, most sacred child, who risked himself beyond measure for his father Nestor when he received the Ethiopian's spear in place of him. But I, your father, do not call you to dangers, but to a service without danger. Will you then grow timid and refuse so easy a help? If so, you would shame the birth-pangs of discourse, the delivery, the midwifery, and that nurture which it provided both in milk and in more solid foods. But this will not happen; therefore neither will the refusal that would produce it.

What, then, is the help which sweet speech, as collector, exacts from you? I have composed a letter to the hallowed Authentria, mother of the holy Despoina, not more for our advantage than for hers, my friend. It asks her to help the canons, which are being oppressed by her all-venerable son, the protostrator - or rather, to help the oppressor himself. For there is nothing terrible for the canons in being pressed. They are a divine thing, his decree and utterance, whose power we believe is perfected in weakness. But for that man the danger is great and heavy, as great as the Lord has set for those who dissolve the ordinances of God.

Whether she acts as an ally of the Son of God, whose laws are being insulted, or as a mother freeing her own son from the injury of injuring others, in any case she gains the greatest things. We therefore ask that this letter be shown through you to the servant of God and our common lady. Since I have also written to the all-venerable protostrator about the same help, let the letter to him also be shown to her, so that through her it may be given to him. For we ask this too of the most holy of women in the letter to her.

Carry out for me this command of service, free from every burden and truly proper to a father sparing the strength of his child, and I shall have received everything owed. You yourself are able to do greater and better things; but may we be kept outside even those trials that seem small and easy to bear, so that we may not trouble either others or you in the least.
