## Revised English Translation

I have learned, most honored brother, that there has now been some change in the affairs concerning us: the man who was formerly strategos has been removed, Eumallos has been sent as registrar of his administration, and Michael, son of lord Polyeuktos, noble both in soul and in ancestral reputation, has now been appointed strategos.

Since these things have happened in this way, and since the Kritopouloi are mine, I write to your sacredness so that they may be guided by you as much as possible, and especially Demetrios. He has more enemies, since he acted in the middle of the matter and could not please everyone. As one of the outside sages declared, not to say the Lord himself, whose saying is better than anything of ours: "Woe to you when all people speak well of you."

Assist them, then, if anything rough should confront them. For everything belonging to the most illustrious lord Eumathios is yours, and yours is his, if love is preserved. Let the episkepititai also be guided by you in all things.

You know, apart from this, the ebb and flow of this life, or rather, to use Scripture's word, its vanity. One of our own sages said: "Do not trouble an already troubled soul." May the Lord give you understanding in all things, and may he cause his fear and longing for him to be rooted in your soul, so that, finding you in the Lord to be every true fruit-bearing vine, as Jeremiah says, he may plant you in his paradise and always visit you with visitation from his holy place.

## Consequential Changes From First Pass
