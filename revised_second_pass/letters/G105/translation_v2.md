## Revised English Translation

Even if the Mother of the Word sends to the height of your Greatness a small and slight gift of fish, nevertheless she sends one that is symbolic and carries in itself something extraordinary and higher than the thoughts of the many. For the one who bore the Word, who made all things in wisdom, must have nothing unwise or irrational.

The very things sent to you display your disposition toward virtue. By that disposition, and through it, you drew up with the hook of discourse whatever was wet in the soul and nourished in the lower waters, where Leviathan is known to exercise his power. Turning it around and bringing it back into yourself, you seasoned it with the salt which we have been appointed to have in ourselves. You melted it down, cured it, and showed it dry and light.

The number of these fish is a symbol of perfection in noble things, being a perfect offspring of the decad, the completion and limit of all numbers. But may you live thus for me and always pass your life in such a way: looking only to God, who came down from above to our lowest place and ascended on high in order to draw up those who are lifted with him, as the promise says; caring little or nothing for the ruler of this world and judging him very poor, even if he boasts that he possesses a kingdom and demands worship in exchange for granting such things.

Be and appear wholly cheerful and a giver of every good, not least to the liturgical order and body of God. By sowing fleshly things for it, you will reap spiritual things, to speak with the divine Apostle, and you will receive God in exchange for the transient things you offer, receiving the one who remains. May you be kept by him above every corruption and injury.
