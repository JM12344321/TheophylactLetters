# Review Prompt

Use this prompt after a draft exists.

```text
You are reviewing an English translation of a letter by Theophylact of Ohrid for philological and Byzantine-historical defensibility.

Materials:
- Gautier ID and metadata.
- Greek source with line references.
- English translation.
- Notes and unresolved questions.

Review tasks:
1. Check every English sentence against the Greek.
2. Identify mistranslated syntax, particles, aspect, voice, or agency.
3. Flag smoothed rhetoric where Theophylact's repetition, antithesis, allusion, or invective matters.
4. Check names, titles, offices, places, and dates against the supplied metadata.
5. Distinguish textual uncertainty from translation uncertainty.
6. Identify where a note is needed for a biblical, classical, legal, fiscal, ecclesiastical, or prosopographical issue.
7. Do not invent missing Greek or manuscript evidence.

Output:
- Required corrections.
- Optional improvements.
- Notes that must be added.
- Remaining blockers before `ready_for_volume`.
```
