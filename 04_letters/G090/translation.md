# G090 Translation

## Metadata

- Recipient: the chartophylax
- Old numbering: Meursius XXXV
- Source: PG 126, public OCR packet PG055, first unit only; the packet continues with another short letter

## English Translation

Holy father and master, let the very fact that I have sent my brother on a long and grievous road in the middle of winter be proof to you of the necessity surrounding us. Against this necessity we need the help of your prayers alone; the rest, as they say, is assistance made of fig-wood.

We have been mixed with so prolific a lot that, although many painful things have befallen me since I began to dwell among the tents of Kedar, the present things surpass those in excess of bitterness by as much as those things surpassed what delights the soul. Now, if ever, I have learned in practice that the apostate and insolent one against God drives us toward abandoning the churches as though we were small-souled, so that he may leap upon them when they are deserted and make sport of divine and heavenly things.

But show help to those who waver through little faith and seem to be drowning in the middle of the sea. Show them the harbor that lies within themselves. Once we come inside the Word, we are set firm, and we are found truly to possess the kingdom of heaven within us. Through everything, teach the one who raises storms against us that he swells the sea in vain, since through you the one who commands the sea is present with us and says, "Be silent, be muzzled"; and within itself its waves will be shattered.

## Translator's Notes

- The source packet continues after this unit with another letter beginning "My lord son"; that second unit is not translated here.
- "Tents of Kedar" evokes Psalm 119/120; "the kingdom within" evokes Luke 17:21; "Be silent, be muzzled" evokes Mark 4:39.
