## English Translation

The murderer of humankind from the beginning, my most radiant son and lord in the Lord, will never cease laying burdens upon my wretchedness. Yet God too does not cease lightening them here through his kindness, so that the Apostle's word is not falsified: he does not allow me to be tested beyond what I am able to bear, but with the temptation provides the way out; and, as David sang, I pass through fire and water and am brought out into refreshment.

The poets too could add verses to my condition. Homer would say that Zeus mixed my life from both jars; Hesiod, shaping us into the middle race, would say that good things have been mingled with evils. When I reckon up the tenfold waves of temptations - I would not call it merely a triple wave - I despair of being saved and rising out of them. But when I look to the pilot who steers my boat with all wisdom, I rise above both despair and the surge.

Consider what is now happening to me concerning the village of the churches. What successive wave does it not surpass? We had not yet escaped the earlier reef - I mean the eunuch; you, standing within the poems, might call him Scylla or Charybdis - when a fresh typhonic wind, the one called Euroclydon, blew upon us, as my poet Luke says.

Yet I have the One who says to the sea, "Be silent, be muzzled," whom even winds obey, who walks on the waters and both commands and gives the same to others. Giving me your hand, you who are skillful for every good and have nothing crooked or leftward, he will save the boat, not only unturned and unsunk, but, wonderful to say, not even washed over.

Truly no small grief came upon us when we learned of the measuring of the village. The Church is always the first to taste the poison, and judgment, according to Scripture, begins from the house of the Lord. Yet when I set against this the thought that all these things lie at your knees, the causes of grief become easier and we become cheerful. Why should I not be comforted, since the matter has been entrusted to your goodness toward us, through which we passed through the former things as well?

Yet I have something to charge you with too. Shall I entrust it to a letter? It would exceed proper measure. When I speak mouth to mouth, I shall bring both the matter itself and the accusation that comes from love. For I will be with you, God guiding my journey, after the synod held here concerning Saint Achilles; and I will see and embrace your most radiant nobility. Before completing that synod it is impossible for us to cross over there, however much we wish it.

May God, who gave you the power to help us now in the matter for which we ask you, also give you greater and still greater power, as you set ascents in your heart and always wish what is good; and may he keep you above every evil and injury.

## Translator's Notes

- The nautical chain - jars, waves, reefs, Euroclydon, Christ stilling the sea - is deliberate and sustained.
- "The village of the churches" likely belongs with the property/fiscal disputes of the surrounding letters.
