## English Translation

So long as only the financial goods of the churches were being plundered, most honored brother and master, we were distressed; nevertheless the burden was lightened elsewhere for us, because we saw that the more spiritual things were not being dissolved, and from that we drew the greatest consolation. But now, when these too are being dissolved for us, every consolation is gone.

For when the divine and sacred canons made nothing more important than preserving each person's dioceses without innovation, and not allowing the conceit of secular authority to slip in among the things of the Spirit; and when those who have been taught to proclaim these canons to others and to correct those who stumble are the very ones who, more than anyone, feed and fatten disobedience toward them, forcing their way into alien dioceses and confounding everything, both divine and human rights - I do not know what other consolation we shall have except the correction of what has been done badly.

Trusting that our zeal will contribute no small aid toward this correction, we make known to your Holiness what is being done contrary to the canons in Kittaba. Do not laugh at me because of the barbarian name. It is a certain parish belonging to our archbishopric. In it one of the monks began raising an oratory, and was stopped by us because he had begun the work contrary to our judgment and was doing things recognized neither by the canons nor by the laws of the polity. He said that he had received a patriarchal stauropegion and had begun the work on that basis. Accordingly he was bound by us with the bonds of exclusion from communion, the penalties fixed by the canons, as you know, having fallen upon him.

Since we do not consider the matter small, we wrote about it to the most honored chartophylax, lord Peter, as much as the measure of a letter allowed. We also write to your God-loving, and therefore canon-loving, Honor, so that you too may speak about this to the man. He knows no less than anyone what has been laid down by the canons and what penalty lies upon those who wish to dissolve them; yet somehow, I do not know how, he permits such things to happen, things by which all good order and peace of the churches are driven away, and the blood of Christ, shed to make all things peaceful, is shown to have been shed in vain.

For what participation does the patriarch of Constantinople have among the Bulgarians, when he has no rights of ordination there, since that region has obtained an autocephalous archbishop, and when he has inherited no other privilege over it? Through whom, then, will the faults of the monks in the monastery established according to his judgment be watched and corrected? Through the one who governs and directs the affairs of his other subject monks, the one we call an exarch? And what man so redolent of Kronos and so moonstruck - to use the comic phrase in tragic circumstances - would bear moderately the presence of a Constantinopolitan exarch among the Bulgarians and not treat him as an adulterer, showing a spirit full of jealousy, as Solomon says? I, for my part, do not think anyone would.

Who else would not be offended even at the report of the lawless deed, at least while piety still walks among us and the canons seem still to breathe in our polity? If our letter and your word accomplish the thing being pursued, thanks be to the God of good order and peace, and thanks also to your love, of which God both is and is said and believed to be God. But if those who are able to correct what is being done badly turn a deaf ear to these things, it will be necessary to cry out more publicly and to speak the pain to more people, in the hope that some may be found who are willing and able to heal it.

Nevertheless, pray for us to have the more undisturbed and peaceful path. We have never desired either to have troubles ourselves or to provide them to others. May this be granted to us now also through your prayers.

## Translator's Notes

- The key issue is jurisdiction: a claimed patriarchal stauropegion inside the autocephalous Bulgarian archbishopric.
- "Kittaba" is left transliterated from the OCR; the place-name needs Gautier's spelling.
