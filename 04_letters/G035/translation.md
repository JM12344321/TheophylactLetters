## English Translation

We passed through fire and water, and you brought us out into refreshment, you who bring out the bound in courage and turn the shadow of death into morning, most honored lord. If the Lord had not been with us, the water would have overwhelmed us and the flame would have burned us up as sinners. But he did not permit the rod of sinners to rest for long upon his inheritance. In our humiliation he remembered us; and, remembering, he blessed us and made this year a year of goodness.

See that you rejoice with me and glorify the One who weaves such things wondrously; and may you yourself also have, in due season, consolation from the newer things that have happened to us. A person is usually persuaded more by things in his own time than by older examples. May the stability also remain with us: that this goodness of God has truly happened. It will remain if we make ourselves worthy of it, since he gives far beyond what we ask, and if we do not use the respite to our condemnation by being persuaded in ease and then kicking through insolent satiety, rising up and playing against ourselves.

These things, I know well, you will fulfill for us because of the law of love. When God grants us time to depart toward the regions of Ioannina, perhaps we shall be united with your sacredness, if it is God's will; and then, seeing one another, we shall heal the longing each has had for the other.

I wished to write to you about your brother, lord Nicholas, asking that you care for him in a more familiar way; but I was ashamed to write to a brother on behalf of a brother, especially to one who knows Paul, over whose Church you preside, bringing fearful condemnation upon those who do not care for their own as they ought. Therefore I refrained, persuaded that, as one nearer to him, you will receive him and deem him worthy of every care, pricked by both nature and reason.

I ask to obtain your prayers as helpers against evil days. For even with these I would scarcely escape, more shamefacedly and more often, the ambushes of evil men, or of the Evil One. As for your part, do not hesitate to give these prayers to us as guides as much as you can. May the Lord be with you to rescue you.

## Translator's Notes

- The letter is a thanksgiving after deliverance, moving quickly into exhortation not to misuse relief.
- "The law of love" is Theophylact's own explanation of why the addressee will rejoice and pray with him.
