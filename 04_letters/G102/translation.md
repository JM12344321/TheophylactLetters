## English Translation

Indeed I do not know whether I should rejoice with you, my child, lord Michael, or grieve with you over your entrance into the palace. The opinion of most people gives us the first; the truth of affairs gives us the second - would that it had not happened. But since what has happened has happened and cannot be undone, prudence is needed, so that the new thing may be handled more skillfully.

This would happen if we paid attention to everything as though living in the midst of scorpions, and if we humbled ourselves beneath those who are, or seem to be, higher. Preconception is a dreadful thing and hard to lever out, as you would say, especially if it is fed and fattened by a malicious and hidden disposition. You know what I mean: "Stranger, move away from tyrants' feet, lest the scepter and thought of God not help you."

So much for that side of things. As for you, do not be indignant at the experience. Steal yourself away as far as you can; descend even to the lowest; be gentle to all; converse mildly and honorably; show earnestness and care toward the sick in your hands. God will make you shine, if I have any skill in conjecture and can draw an inference. Let your tongue be of good report. May the Lord keep you above every corruption and injury.

## Translator's Notes

- The advice to Michael is courtly and pastoral: humility, caution, and diligent medical service in the palace.
