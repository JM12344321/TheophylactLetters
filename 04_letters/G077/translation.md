## English Translation

Your letters came to me, most honored master, and their power descended upon my mind as snow upon green grass or rain upon pasture. Your conversation was sweet to me, seasoned with the grace and salt of brotherly love. I embraced you as though you were present and kissed my master and father with a mouth of love.

Ah, Envy, how have you separated us from one another by such long walls, so that you might sting each of us in isolation, and there might be "woe to the one" in turn and again to the other, and so that the cord between us might not become unbreakable by being intertwined? Blessed be God, the Word of God, who by the power of speech makes those far away near, and by the grace of letters proves the contrivances of Envy false. For even if we have been orphaned from one another in face, we have not been orphaned in heart. We speak with one another through the mouth of letters, and from the burning heat of sorrows each cools the other, taking up echo's work and returning the words received about our oppressors. Even so we lighten ourselves by the utterances of speech from the heavy spirit of the heart.

Since, then, you first drank to me the cup of narration about the things around you, I in turn pledge you such a cup: may the vine perish from which such drinks come to us. Sennacherib the Assyrian crushed us, the one sent against us without mercy in the land between the rivers. But the second was heavier by far, insofar as he was more foolish; for folly, among its other evils, makes those whom it feeds unbroken and unyielding in wickedness. Because I resisted him on behalf of our own inward parts, if indeed those who later plotted against us must still be called inward parts, and because I spoke a few words on behalf of the people being cut down and divided up, he fell upon me, devoured me, tore me apart, filled me with bitterness, and made me drunk with gall.

In a word, anyone who has said or done something against the Church is honored by him; anyone denounced for speaking with us, or even for entering the church on a feast, is dishonored and treated worse than what villain? He even begrudged Christians this much, so that under a common pretext he might hide his own behavior. For he himself did not even think it worth his while to venerate the Mother of God. In his own opinion he was protecting his unbowed and unyielding attitude toward us - so deranged has he become. As I judge it, he was only suffering what he deserved, and even there he had his punishment, from the very things by which he is mad: he knows neither how to honor human beings nor God, but has become wholly possessed by the frenzy that has slipped into him.

From this, how do you think all of us here were affected? Some were only sent away; others actually rose against us; but wickedness, simply put, turned a somersault over everyone. For us Jesus remained - both the saving reality and the saving name. Holding fast to him, when the others were scattered, we endured what we were suffering. He whispered to us that sweet voice: "Take courage; I have overcome the world." With this voice even now he shepherds us; he makes me dwell in a green place and nourishes me beside water of rest.

What more? The enemy, a murderer from the beginning, was not satisfied with the nearby wounds and blows; he shot against us an episcopal arrow. I hinted at this in an earlier letter to your Holiness. He raised against me the bishop of such-and-such a city, a man who is both worker and workshop of every evil craft. He nearly put me among those who go down into the grave, and made that man - alas for my evils - a murderer.

To dramatize the story of that man would be longer than my leisure allows and more painful than your freedom from pain can bear. I do not wish you to have sorrow upon sorrow, since your brotherly love extends no less to him than to me. For according to the wise admonition, the one who does evil is the one who suffers evil; for this reason he is most pitiable to you, and speech about him would be more grievous. I shall stop speaking about this, lest we insult the high priesthood twice: by what they have done, things not even fit to mention, and by what we say, things that ought not even to be imagined. Such things must be buried, lest we give the sufferings of the people a wider field.

Besides, having made terms with a brother, even if he remains implacable and marked with his variegated stains, I would act inconsistently if I stirred the disgust up again. Yet, wishing to show your Honor dimly the causes of our griefs, I have recalled so much of them.

Because of these things I must set out to the emperor, which is the same as saying that I must measure deadly Charybdis again, or pass through Egypt: a hard and grievous road. Do not think, man of God, that I consider this road lighter than any of the afflictions I have undergone, or that what seems to be a solution of difficulties does not seem to me a more unbreakable bond. It is not only that a bishop approaching the military camp is an object of envy or hatred in these sinful days. Nor is it only that our horses must foal three times a year, like the Elysium of myth which we learned when we were in the swaddling bands of education and then taught when we swaddled others, so that we may mount everyone with whom we speak; nor that our fields must produce a harvest of gold, or even that trees must put forth golden fruit like the apples of the Hesperides in poetry. For if such things are not provided by any one of us, and if we cannot tend these bitter men with the desired soothing gifts, we are blasphemed as buriers - would that those who say such things were buried instead.

These things make the road to the camp steep, narrow, and constricted for us, and not even good at its end. So too does this cobweb body of mine, which, as you know, is weak and easily dissolved for every labor, especially the labor of roads. But if you gird yourself with strength and fasten around us the power of your prayers, everything will become easier for us, since God performs the will of those who fear him, making a road for us through your angelic prayers and casting the stones out of the way.
