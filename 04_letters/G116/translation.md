## English Translation

My brother is enough to make our affairs known to you, my surpassingly brilliant son in the Lord - if indeed for the moment you need someone to make them known, and do not yourself already know them and make them known to others, as one who learned them long ago. Unless someone should say that you know their quality but not their magnitude. Yet even this it is not likely that you are ignorant of, since you know how the injuries now rise toward something greater; for from my own affairs I reason about yours as well.

Against all things I call upon the Lord as helper. May you be kept by him above every injury and corruption.
