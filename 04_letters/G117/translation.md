## English Translation

Do not be surprised if I write to you in Laconic fashion. If you are surprised, ask the bearer of the letter and you will learn the reason for the brevity. It is the bearer himself. He showed himself almost winged for the approach to you on our behalf, and so asked for the letters.

But be healthy for me and be cheerful. May the sickness of grief not overtake you; may you not need medicines, even such medicines as your tongue has hymned as belonging to love.

## Translator's Notes

- The joke is that the messenger's haste causes the letter's brevity.
