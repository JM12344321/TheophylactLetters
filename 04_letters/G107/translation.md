- Old numbering: Meursius LII
- Source: PG 126, public OCR packet PG069; aligned by GIBI index

## English Translation

You continually renew by your deeds the incarnation of God. You show that in you he becomes human, and that in him your human being is made divine. You condescend to all, and you were pleased to visit even me, a man who is very earth itself. You made the earth drunk with goodness, and you gave life and resurrection to your own man, who was otherwise dead.

In return for these things, what greater prayer could I offer you than that you never cease showing the divine mystery in yourself, so that the same thing may be for you both achievement and reward?

## Translator's Notes

- This is a compact thanksgiving for a visit during illness.
- The theology of condescension and deification is unusually compressed but clear.
