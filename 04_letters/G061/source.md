- Identification: Margaret Mullett cites Meursius I as Gautier `G61`, locates it at PG 126, col. 357, and elsewhere refers to `G61` as addressed to `?John Komnenos`. The OCR unit begins with `ΕΠΙΣΤΟΛΗ ΠΡΩΤΗ` and ends before `ΕΠΙΣΤ. Β' Τῷ μαΐστορι κυρίῳ Ἰωάννῃ`.
- Primary draft source for this pass: PG 126 / Calfa-GREgORI OCR, `02_sources/pg126/PG126_text.txt`, lines around 8875-8928.
- Modern control source still required: Paul Gautier, *Theophylacte d'Achrida: Lettres*, CFHB 16/2 (1986), for recipient, date, letter boundaries, Greek text, apparatus, and notes.
- The Greek in `greek.txt` is a working reconstruction from OCR, not an edited critical text. Do not cite it without Gautier collation.
- Major OCR/collation trouble spots: the clause about public affairs and the `demosion/demos`; the Homeric line around Lycurgus; the phrase about the lake; and the closing blessing after `Καταβὰς, φησίν, ὄψομαι`.
