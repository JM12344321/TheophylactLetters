## English Translation

Through your very great assistance, my all-venerable lord, we have obtained moderate rest, at least in the few things remaining to the Church around Ohrid. You were going to accomplish something by charming our tax official on behalf of our weakness. Therefore we do not cease bringing to your incomparable goodness what we have: to God, prayers, as his ministers; to human beings, thanks and praise.

Even if praise matters little to you because of the awareness of human weakness which reason has worked in you, do not cease taming for us this desert of groaning by your continual wonders on our behalf. I count your works for our Church as charms by which the aims of the tax officials at each moment are blunted. If you had not stood as our helper, nothing would prevent us from being consumed by the serpents, many of which this desert nourishes. They are made alive and fattened by the permission from above which our sins bring upon us.

In this way the just Judge and awarder will weave more crowns for you in the day of repayment, keeping you here also above every injury and corruption.
