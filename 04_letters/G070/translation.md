## English Translation

Has even the interpreter of the truth of the Gospel been caught lying? Or shall we call your action something else, and not a lie? We shall call it, of course, a dispensation arranged more wisely: through praises, as though through hands, it lifted up our childishness as it rolled on the ground because of disgust with circumstances, and with the brighter kisses of a loving mouth and the stroking of encomia it stopped our whimpering.

This is how I judge the matter. I would not readily suppose that you, who guide others toward truth, lie so easily, any more than I would suppose that one who enlightens others is darkened. Nor would I suppose that you so disregard our affairs as not to know in what condition they are. Knowing this, it remains that you must help, and help in the way possible to you: through the power of speech. Am I then to vote against the verdict that reasonably turns aside from us, unless the evil men are powerless to trip up our judgment in this one thing too?

If something deeper has been examined by you, and for this reason you have flowed abundantly in praises against us, having received in a rush the voluntary rain of love from above, explain this to us as well, you who reveal the depths of God to the divine people through the Spirit. Still, however the matter stands, it seems to me wholly alien to the present days. In them, because lawlessness has multiplied and love has grown cold, we bite and devour one another. It is extremely difficult to meet not only a praiser, but even a moderate reviler. A good man among us is not the one who gives gracious gifts, but the one who lavishly gives the gift of not harming; and simply blessed is not the man divine in virtue, but the man not demonic in vice.

You, then, keep straining forward in this way, prosper, and differ from the bad; for it is possible. My brothers, who are your students, reverence your honor, embrace you in the truest way one could say, and promise to observe your command. Only pray for them and for us too an escape from the temptations that distract us.

## Translator's Notes

- The opening answers exaggerated praise from Niketas by interpreting it as pastoral "economy" rather than falsehood.
