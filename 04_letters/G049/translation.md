## English Translation

You, good man, are so anxious on behalf of my words, lest they be caught in falsehood, that you have hastened to confirm them by the deeds themselves. For I was always accusing you of negligence and saying that you disowned your calling, as though even Autolykos would not disown Odysseus.

But, blessed man, do not honor friendship in this way. What you would wish for yourself, wish also for the one who is thought to be loved. If the things you undertook have been completed, good. If not, at least this will lay up a very great favor with us: that you not also lose the rights that were given to you.

Worthless as I am, I was asking another thing of you as well: when the decree concerning Vodena is made, show it to lord Gregory Taronites and ask from him a note to the man acting in Beroia, indicating what he must do. If the decree has been made, the request concerning this still stands. If not, see that, being so swift, you do not also run the risk of stumbling. We have learned from the proverb that speed is unsafe.

May the Lord keep you from every snare of the devil and from negligence toward good things.

## Translator's Notes

- The letter is practical and procedural: decree, transmission, and local enforcement.
- Vodena and Beroia require historical/place-name verification in the final apparatus.
