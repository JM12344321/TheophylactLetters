## English Translation

We are not unaware that you are grieving and that, unable to endure the flames of brotherly affection, you are bitten in soul. As for us, struck by the goads of friendship, we too are in pain; our soul is doubly distressed, because our friend is sorrowing and because, being far away, we cannot be present with you or bring you words of consolation.

For this reason, though absent, we speak with you as far as we can through this letter, and we exhort your good, brother-loving, and God-loving soul: whenever the loss of that man bites your soul, take great consolation from this. Consider that your brother has not died, but has gone abroad to God. Freed from a painful life and from illnesses - by which, if as a human being he had contracted any stain, he was wholly purified - he went away to the Lord full of grace.

What sort of life his was, not you alone but all of us know.
