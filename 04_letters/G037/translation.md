## English Translation

How could I have passed over the letter-carrier himself when he was present, most holy father, and used another deacon for my letters to you, when I know this man is united to us by the unbreakable bonds of the Spirit? I saw him, and was three times glad.

This desert through which we have been condemned to rub our lives away, as though we had turned back in our desires toward Egypt, is a good nurse of serpents and scorpions. As for human beings, wickedness does not truly know how to bring forth anything good; and even if it should receive some imported good, it transforms it into itself. For these reasons I saw the brother gladly, thirsty for such sights, and above all because he knows your affairs, which he reported to us. We longed intensely to learn these things, and, in David's phrase, we fainted with this desire; above all we thirsted to hear about our most holy and thrice-blessed father.

For the report that he desired to be enclosed again disturbed us not a little, since he pressed the matter more violently and ardently. How, O common stumbling-block of those running well, did you contrive this against the holy soul and attempt, alas, to sink a cargo-ship full of many good wares just at the harbor itself, as you imagined, by confusing the calm sea of the brotherhood? But the spirit of uprightness and goodness blew back against you, set the waves against you, and when the sacred anchor and the flesh had been cast, the danger was turned upon your own head. Now he has fallen from whatever place he occupied, while everything is full of calm and the Lord's tunic remains undivided.

When we learned of the father's rest, we rejoiced and were very glad, and because we had become an object of mockery and derision to those around us for not setting many people on fire with the scandal by helping their own causes from the bruises inflicted on the monks.

But because I was so delighted by your affairs, I forgot my own, and so failed to weep them out to you. I was captured to excess by brotherly joy and led away as an untyrannized prisoner. I have only this much to say about our circumstances: if the Lord had not been with us, those who contrive sinful additions upon our backs would have swallowed us alive, men who know nothing of judgment and mercy, as Solomon says.

May we not be deprived, by your prayers, of the Lord who wrestles against these men. May he be with us until the works of this evil age are completed, according to the great promise. May the Holy One of Israel, who guards our affairs, neither slumber nor sleep. For unless he guards, we shall keep watch in vain.

The days are evil. Every brother trips his neighbor by the heel; a man's household are his enemies; the collectors are always setting traps for us. Contempt has truly been poured out upon rulers; the reverent man has perished from the earth; everyone snatches hides from flesh; each builds his own Zion with plunder and injustices; and the leech has displayed not three daughters, but countless daughters. So fertile has she now become, always conceiving pains against us and bringing forth lawlessness.

Because of these things I have become like a terebinth that has shed its leaves and like a garden without water. I converse with books only once in a year; I meditate instead on the anxieties caused by those who wrong us and on how I might soften them with stronger inducements. For fear of them reigns, while the fear of God, as they say, is far away.

We need your prayers, for you long ago recognized our weakness. I greet all your sons, my brothers, whom you have presented as sons of light to the Father of lights, making them fit to stand in the portion of the inheritance of the saints [the PG/OCR packet breaks off here].
