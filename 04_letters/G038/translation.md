## English Translation

I have seen Ethiopian fathers looking upon their own children as though they were, as people say, offspring of the sun, and clinging more gladly to their ugliness than to beauty if it belonged to someone else. In the same way I have seen judges cherishing their own votes, even when they were not tightly girded to the laws, as though they had been drawn from tablets of Zeus, and persisting in their decisions with intense resolve.

If they truly bring forth just votes, and if the scales of their decisions belong to Minos or Rhadamanthys, even when they judge men slaughtered for death, does this not seem to your quick and great mind very splendid? I think it does, unless you wish to wrong your own truth.

Since, then, these things have been set down and acknowledged by me, let neither the devices and evil arts of those opposed to us terrify you, nor let your hands be slack or your heart poured out because they bend their bow against you, bitter thing though that is. Rather, be manly, and let your heart be strengthened. The Lord is just, and he has loved righteousness. Perhaps he will break their bows, and no scourge will come near your tent. But if they do strike, their blows will be counted as arrows of infants.

Stand for me upon the rock, and, trusting in the things I judged justly, defend them, holding before you the shield of speech, whose matter God supplied beforehand by nature and whose form your labor has shaped. If the acts done concerning the village need to be reported to the emperor, we are not aware that you yourselves have dissolved them. Let your nobility do this no less readily than one would raise the sun, unless you say that this is the obstacle to the report: that the act must be entered into the record. That indeed was the very thing we sought most thirstily. Once this happens, we shall rejoice three times over and embrace the memorandum.

Therefore, unless you yourself supply the cause of the disturbances being brought upon you, as they said, and unless after doing what was proper we have somehow slipped, gird your sword upon your thigh, mighty one. Shut in those who pursue me from the opposite side, and God will gird you with strength in every time, in every form of life and circumstance.

Concerning the kind of matter about which you wrote to me, it would have been better if you had written earlier. May the Lord be your refuge and strength.

## Translator's Notes

- The legal imagery is strong: votes, scales, mythic judges, recorded acts, and the shield of speech.
- The property dispute behind "the village" should be coordinated with G031 and nearby administrative letters.
