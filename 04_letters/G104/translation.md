## Metadata

- Gautier ID: G104
- Recipient: John Attaliates, protonotary of the duke of Attaleia
- Old numbering: Meursius XLIX
- Source: PG 126, public OCR packet PG067; aligned by GIBI index

## English Translation

I think you owe me thanks, my most brilliant son in the Lord, because while you were still young in age I brought you to the divine graces of the Muses, preserved you, and nurtured you. You yourself would not deny this debt; otherwise you would show yourself a stranger to the Muses.

Since, then, you acknowledge it, discharge the whole debt to me by taking up the cause of the most holy metropolitan of Side. He is so united to me that he seems to be no other than I, but to be myself. Whatever power the good God has given you for doing good, stir it all up for me so that good may be done to this wholly noble and good servant of God.

Yes, my most kind son: may I thus enjoy the fruits of my labors over you; may I thus receive from you the payments owed for nurture. When I learn from his own letter how much and what kind of guidance he enjoys from you, may it be mine to bless you with prayers - if nothing else, at least fatherly prayers, prayers able to establish the houses of children. As I always do this, may I find the true Father of the true Word attentive, keeping you above every injury and corruption.

## Translator's Notes

- The letter is a recommendation on behalf of the metropolitan of Side.
- The teacher/student debt is cast as repayment for formation in the Muses.
