## English Translation

I have not yet been cleanly released from the sickness that laid me down, my most distinguished son in the Lord. Nevertheless the voice of the canons, summoning us to the sacred synod - a voice that is certainly Christ's - raises me from the bed, strengthens me for movement, and enables me to carry the pallet.

Receive us, then, together with the one who performs such wonders, at Prespa, for the assembly is there. We shall embrace you, cling to you, and enjoy your filial disposition. But see that you do not leave unsupported the ecclesiastical man sent to gather what is needed. Otherwise you will prove those who are going to sing the feast to be mere cicadas, and at the festival, while they are intoxicated, we shall starve.

Then where under the earth will you sink down in order to escape the missiles of speech? You have not yet, even today, exposed yourself to them, because you are truly moved according to reason. But others who have tried them report that they are bitter. Leaving such people to fall under them again, as men accustomed to them and deserving them, may you yourself be showered with flowers by this speech. That, after all, is both natural to you and has grown up together with you.

## Translator's Notes

- The letter asks Makrembolites to arrange practical support for the synod at Prespa.
- The pallet image alludes to Christ's healing command.
