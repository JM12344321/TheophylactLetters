## English Translation

Even if you have seen that Alcmaeon, the man believed to be received by no part of the earth because of his matricide, when you see me you will behold that unhappy man's misery in my case. For the executioner who obstructs human prosperity and harvests human calamities drove me from the land in Ohrid; then, perceiving that I had fled toward Pelagonia, he does not think it right for me to tread that land either. He has no claim of justice to put forward, only that he is a public executioner.

Why, then, do I lament these things to you? So that you yourself may put an end to my wandering. Certainly the stream of your goodness is no smaller than that of Achelous, who supplied new land for Alcmaeon. If you ask the way in which we shall be set firm, you will learn it from my brother, whom we have sent for this very purpose: to find a solution for our misfortunes - for some through others, but for this dishonor through your skill. Show me, then, even in this suffering, the physician Paean.

## Translator's Notes

- Alcmaeon found refuge on newly formed land, often associated with Achelous; Theophylact recasts that myth as a plea for safe standing ground.
