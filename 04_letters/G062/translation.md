## English Translation

Neither I nor the circumstances in which we have been have had much leisure. If leisure were with us, the matter might perhaps have been painful, since something possible was not happening. But since neither is possible, it is truly foolish to seek what cannot be done. Let us will the other thing, I mean that he, though cut off from us, may enjoy relief.

Yet if this proves false to our love for him, and shows us lovers of ourselves rather than lovers of our brother, then we have fallen so far from the commandment, which orders us to love our neighbor above ourselves, that we do not even fulfill the measure of love required by the Law, which gives the neighbor equality with ourselves. And this in the case of one who loved us above himself and showed it by the things in which he was worn out, so that we might pass our life in ease.

Besides, how is it not absurd to be attached to him because he had faith toward us, on account of which he exposed himself to every danger, while we ourselves do not trust the common Master and confidently entrust to him the course left to us in life, believing that with God it will be lighter and have nothing rough or steep? The one who cares for us is the same one who then cared for us through him. He is the same and has not changed. He will shepherd us, and nothing will be lacking to us.

When Moses came to rest, did Israel remain without a guardian? Did they not receive as guide and general the type of the greater one, the servant of greater things and fulfiller of the promise concerning the desired land, of which Moses had not been deemed worthy? When Elijah was wondrously taken up, did not Elisha appear as a prophet with a double portion of the Spirit?

I believe that our affairs will henceforth be better than before, so that we may learn that we were not being carried through by human thoughts and efforts, but unexpectedly by divine goodness and words of power. It is likely that you, thinking these and more than these, sit above faint-heartedness and the lowliness of such thoughts. These reflections, when they came into my mind, allowed me to rise again from the collapse caused by what happened.

Hold yourself so, most honored brother, and do not let the arrows of children playing their games, with which they take aim against us, shake the tower of your understanding. As a wise man, you have already founded it upon the rock; and the rock is Christ. The winds will blow, and the rivers will rush upon it through rain and permission from above. But let that rock remain firm and unshaken for you, so that you may boast in Christ and say, "Our soul passed through the torrent. Did our soul then pass through the unsubstantial water?"

May the God of all consolation himself console you more perfectly through the Paraclete, the Spirit equal in power.

## Translator's Notes

- The letter is consolatory, apparently after the loss or removal of a protector.
- The "torrent/unsubstantial water" allusion is Psalm 123/124.
